What you'll do

Financial tracking & budgeting

  • Log, categorize and reconcile weekly expenses in Google Sheets
  • Audit receipts, utility statements and subscriptions for savings
  • Prepare monthly budget vs. actual reports

Inventory & procurement

  • Keep live counts of household and project supplies
  • Set reorder thresholds so nothing runs out
  • Compare vendor pricing, track packages, handle returns

Scheduling & digital organization

  • Manage and audit shared Google Calendars
  • Learn to schedule your own appointments: call or book online, confirm the details, add each one to your Google Calendar and invite your supervisor as a guest
  • Keep your Google Calendar shared with your supervisor so all appointments stay visible
  • Organize Drive folders using YYYY-MM-DD_Vendor_Description naming
  • Sort incoming email and flag priority items

Special projects & SOPs

  • Request vendor quotes and schedule maintenance
  • Support event and travel logistics
  • Write standard operating procedures for routine work
